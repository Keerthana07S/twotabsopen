who's behind the cans

A note from our founder.

My elementary school teachers didn't see much in me.

I was the AuDHD kid who couldn't sit still and couldn't do things "the right way," and for a long time I believed that meant something was wrong with me.

Nobody had a plan for a brain like mine, so slowly, I started making my own.

It took five or six years and a lot of help along the way. Today I study aerospace engineering at Princeton, I've been lucky enough to do research with MIT, and I run a nonprofit that mentors girls in STEM. I'm still figuring plenty out, but I know what it feels like to be underestimated.

I started Two Tabs Open to be the person I needed back then. If you've ever felt written off, you're in the right place.

— founder, Two Tabs Open
